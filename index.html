Your first sign-in

HR creates your account, so you don't sign yourself up. Your first visit takes about two minutes.

  1. HR gives you a usernameYou also get a temporary password. Both come from HR, not by email.
  2. Sign in with themYou'll land on a short profile page instead of the home screen.
  3. Add your details, pick a passwordBirthday, contact number, address and an emergency contact. Then the rest of Hive opens.

Everyone sees only what they should

Access depends on your role. Employees never see anyone else's payslip, leave or personal details.

Employee

Your own record, nothing more.

  • Clock in and out, request attendance corrections
  • Leave balance, the monthly attendance bonus and requests
  • Your own payslips, once HR releases them
  • Other employees' records or the admin pages

HR Admin

The whole team, with every change logged.

  • Create employee accounts and manage the directory
  • Approve leave and attendance corrections
  • Edit payslips, run payroll and re-open it to fix errors
  • Post announcements and export reports
